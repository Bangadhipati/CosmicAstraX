About Project & Vision

CosmicAstraX is an advanced, hardware-accelerated 3D Orrery and celestial mechanics simulator crafted by Team AstraGenX under the direction of Debarghya Bhowmick, initially created for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2024.

Analytical Orbital Engine

Unlike pre-rendered animations, CosmicAstraX solves Kepler's Equation (M = E - e sin E) at 60 FPS using an analytical Newton-Raphson orbital solver spanning from 3000 BC to 3000 AD.

Orbital Elements Semi-major axis, eccentricity, inclination, mean longitude, perihelion argument, and ascending node.
Deep-Space Horizon View calibrated up to 1,400 AU, rendering extreme bodies from asteroid 1566 Icarus to dwarf planet 90377 Sedna.
Trajectory Highlighting Focused celestial body glows radiant cyan/gold while ambient paths soften.
Verified Scientific Telemetry

Continuous data baselines are sourced directly from verified global space agencies and research institutes:

NASA JPL Horizons High-precision planetary ephemerides and physical constants.
ISRO Open Data Lunar telemetry, planetary research, and deep-space mission benchmarks.
NeoWs Service Near Earth asteroid trajectories and close-approach metrics.
Astronomy Engine Microsecond ephemeris math and Three.js WebGL graphics core.
Navigation & Controls
Left Click + Drag Orbit and rotate perspective around celestial targets.
Scroll / Pinch Smooth deep-space zoom across the heliosphere.
Timeline HUD Scrub time forward/backward, jump to custom dates, and modulate rates.
Universal Search Instant autocomplete lookup for planets, moons, asteroids, and missions.
